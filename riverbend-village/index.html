🌊 Riverbend Village

It is a dry season. The well is low. Neighbors worry about water for drinking, cooking, and the farm.

You are a young community inventor. Your challenge:

Help Riverbend Village get enough clean water while keeping the community healthy, fair, and happy.

💡 Great inventors begin by noticing what people need. Explore the village, listen carefully, then build a solution.

Villagers are friendly fictional characters in a learning game — not real people.

    ❓ How the Village Works

    Each simulated day, weather changes and the village uses water.

    • 🏠 Homes and school need drinking water every day.
    • 🌾 The farm needs water to grow food.
    • 🌧️ Rain collectors work best on rainy days.
    • 🛢️ Tanks save water for dry days.
    • 🧪 Filters keep water clean — without them, stream water can lower health.
    • ☀️ Solar pumps move stream water, but overuse can harm the stream.
    • 💧 Drip irrigation helps the farm use less water (not for drinking).
    • 🔧 Repair benches help machines stay working.
    • 🤝 Fair sharing improves Trust. Unfair sharing lowers it.
